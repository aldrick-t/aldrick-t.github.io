This grouped item collects five competency credentials issued by Tecnológico de Monterrey in connection with the Ingeniería en Robótica y Sistemas Digitales curriculum. Each credential reports a `validFrom` timestamp of July 25, 2026 and states that evidence was presented demonstrating the integrated development of the named competency.

### Disciplinary competencies

- **Embedded Systems** — Develops embedded systems that comply with quality, safety, and performance standards.
- **Smart Interfaces** — Creates hardware and software interfaces that enable intelligent interaction between digital devices.
- **Cyber-physical Systems** — Demonstrates integrated disciplinary and transversal competencies in the Cyber-physical Systems concentration. The credential identifies capabilities in digital culture, process automation, technological-design project management, automation proposals, embedded-system implementation, complex-problem solution implementation, evaluation of emerging technologies, and decision-making.
- **Intelligent Robotic and Digital Systems** — Develops the intelligence components that enable a robot to act autonomously when solving problems.

The three credentials connected to the degree curriculum reference the 2019 study plans and transversal competencies in self-awareness and management, innovative entrepreneurship, social intelligence, ethical and civic commitment, reasoning for complexity, communication, and digital transformation.

### Transversal competency

- **Social Intelligence** — Generates effective collaboration and negotiation environments in multicultural contexts with respect and appreciation for the diversity of people, knowledge, and cultures. The credential emphasizes collaboration, respect for diversity, and negotiation effectiveness.
